# Demo Flow

1. Open the Streamlit dashboard.
2. Enter GPS coordinates or upload GPS CSV.
3. Upload sample satellite/terrain CSV.
4. Enter land area, slope, rainfall and water index.
5. View the prototype priority score.
6. Enter equipment power, operating hours and days.
7. View estimated energy requirement.
8. Explain that the production version will connect live GIS, satellite and mobile camera data.
