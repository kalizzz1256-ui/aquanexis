Ridge Treatment (Slope > 10%)

Continuous Contour Trenches (CCT) and vegetative barriers intercept rainfall velocity right where it drops, preventing gully initiation and encouraging in-situ moisture retention.

Drainage Line Treatment (Stream Order 1-2)

Loose Boulder Structures (LBS) and wire-mesh Gabion dams stabilize slopes, reduce kinetic energy, filter silt, and prevent deepening of erosion channels.

Water Harvesting (Valley Floor)

Check dams and percolation tanks create surface storage reserves and recharge deep confined aquifers, extending post-monsoon well yield for local agriculture.
